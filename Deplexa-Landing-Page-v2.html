The learning toolkit

More than a place to save words.

Deplexa brings useful learning tools together, helping you move from collecting vocabulary to understanding and using it.

01 / BUILD

Your vocabulary, your way.

Save useful words, add meanings and examples, and grow a personal collection that stays organised.

02 / HEAR

Get closer to pronunciation.

Work with pronunciation, phonetic details and audio to become more familiar with spoken English.

03 / TEST

Learn by putting it to use.

Review what you have collected with quizzes and activities built around your vocabulary.

04 / CONNECT

See how words relate.

Explore word families, meanings and connections that make vocabulary easier to understand.

05 / EXPLORE

Understand English in context.

Build a stronger feel for words through explanations, examples and practical language learning.

06 / GROW

Make learning a habit.

Return to your words, keep practising and make steady progress at your own pace.
